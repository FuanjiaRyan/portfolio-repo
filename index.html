BioKit App

A lab guide app to help Advance Level Biology students with fast and reliable access to their lab manual diagrams and answers. This app contains all the necessary diagrams for the GCE Advance Level manual with all its labelling and classification.

Flutter Dart Firebase Figma

HypeLink App

An app built by my team and I during The CIMFEST hackathon that took place in November 2025. This app allows artists to interact with fans. Artists post their videos or songs in the app and fans can see and buy them serving as a source of income to both fans and artists as NFT functionality was implemented. I was the frontend and backend developer in my group.

Flutter Dart Firebase NFT
